Implementation model

  1. Define a Pune-specific economic question.
  2. Collect ethical, documented primary and public data.
  3. Analyse distribution, costs and incentives.
  4. Use AI to predict, classify, group or optimise where it adds value.
  5. Evaluate potential impact and communicate limitations.

Recommended framing: AI supports evidence and decisions; a student prototype does not by itself solve a municipal service problem.

Suggested first-batch shortlist

Water tanker economy · Traffic congestion prediction · Economic cost of congestion · Garbage generation · Waste segregation behaviour · Air pollution prediction · Rental prices · Graduate skill gaps · Civic complaint hotspots · Vegetable price prediction.

Expected team outputs

Problem statement · documented dataset · economic analysis · evaluated AI prototype · impact and feasibility assessment · policy brief · presentation or dashboard.

Geographic examples are suggestions for fieldwork. Validate local conditions and data access before treating any issue as confirmed for a specific neighbourhood.
